# Digital Gardens of North Tyne — corrected research release (9 November 2026)

This source/data directory accompanies the *Energy & Environmental Science* Analysis manuscript:
**Digital Gardens of North Tyne versus grid-supplied AI campuses: energy, carbon, heat, computing and community benefits**.

## Essential correction

The prior model used a cubic power ramp from cut-in to rated wind speed. `power_247.py` now converts hourly ERA5 100 m wind to output using a documented, *generic* modern 4–5 MW turbine shape, with cut-in 3 m/s, 20% gross rated at 6 m/s, 48% at 8 m/s, 90% at 10 m/s, rated at 12 m/s and cut-out at 25 m/s, followed by a 12% aggregate deduction for wakes, availability and electric losses. It is **not** a manufacturer or observed Ray/Middlemoor power curve. Raw ERA5 fleet CF = 35.02%; the primary case is speed-calibrated to 30% fleet CF.

## Main result

The central least-cost selected portfolio satisfying modelled 80% renewable hourly self-supply is **400 MW wind, 800 MWp solar, 100 MWh battery**. Historical 2018–2025 year-paired self-supply is 80.503%; selected supply-energy model cost is £101.091/MWh, conditional on 1,021.04 GWh/yr exports at the declared hourly price capture. With *unscaled* ERA5 speeds, the separately optimised alternative is **300 MW wind, 900 MWp solar, 100 MWh**, cost £98.937/MWh. The user-supplied former 250/1200/400 result is superseded and must not be used in a fresh paper.

The partial delivered-energy proxy (non-commodity add-ons based on initial model assumptions) is £196.53/MWh for DG vs £166.30/MWh for same-IT, modelled grid-campus case. Full tariff rates, exports and connection costs remain uncontracted and site-specific. Selected environmental scenarios and food/heat/vehicle ledgers are conditional.

## Reproduce numerical outputs (from RESEARCH folder)

Run in a standard Python environment with NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib, Requests, python-dateutil and other packages imported by the included modules. Source data are included in `DATA/`, original weather/provider archive metadata is maintained, and derived numerical results in `OUTPUT/`.

```bash
python power_247.py design
python power_247.py
python design_trajectory.py
python trajectory.py
python heat_community.py
python island_network.py
python export_scenarios.py
python design_econ.py
python compare_econ.py
python revised_sensitivities.py
python spectrum_xgs_screen.py
python validate.py
python figures.py
```

**Reproduction performed in this workspace:** all above core scripts, including the full 16-year weather/dispatch design search and 25-year trajectory, completed. The current `validate.py` passed **144 consistency assertions**. This verifies the model's internal calculations from its prepared inputs; it is not verification of raw weather provider downloads, true local generation, physical distribution-network security, or project permits.

The `build_manuscript.py` script is a historical manuscript *starting-point* generator, not a byte-exact reproduction of the final editorially revised RSC `Article.docx`; the final article has additional calibrated methods, economic limits and Spectrum-XGS discussions. Numerical outputs are generated by the analysis scripts above, and the final journal documents are located in the outer archive's `SUBMIT/` folder.

## Field validation extensions

Optional `optional_field_validation.py` reads **user-supplied** Ofgem REGO measured monthly station output and compares it to modelled wind proxies after month matching. `download_pvlive.py` tries to request Sheffield Solar PV_Live regional solar estimates; it has not been verified against a live endpoint. No REGO station series, parish-level Census/heating microdata, licensed network connection price or site-measured river flow series was retrieved during this revision. Use script help and provider terms before running.

## Other limitations

- 100 MWh battery = 25 MW four-hour power rating, not instant replacement for a 55 MW backup turbine outage.
- Fuel-only 15.4-day autonomy disables renewables and imports; it is deterministic across weather years and lacks start-up delay, dynamic stability and N-1 protection tests.
- Annual resident benefits are *proposals*: 146 kg vegetables/produce and 46 kg fish/prawns per person; vehicles and free charging; subsidised farmer HVO; biogas, heat and drying. No implementation contracts are supplied.
- Vehicle manufacturing emissions (8–12t per vehicle over 12y) are gross screening assumptions; future conventional baselines can decarbonise and credits should not be multiplied by 25 unchanged.
- 400/800 Gb/s Spectrum-XGS scale-across *lower-bound* transfer times assume 70% payload and 5 μs/km fibre propagation; actual all-reduce throughput, latency stability and model training have not been tested.
- The environmental label is a certification **proposal**; Dolphin Safe's observed consumer retail price effects and cloud storage survey responses cannot be used directly as AI-GPU willingness-to-pay premiums.
- No patents or patent applications are pending on this project (author confirmation). Potential financial interest in the proposal is disclosed in the article.
- Data/provider licence conditions should be checked before public redistribution of individual source downloads; the complete archive is intended for study and referee review.
